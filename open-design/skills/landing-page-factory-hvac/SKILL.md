# landing-page-factory-hvac

Follow the **landing-page-factory** base contract. Trade-services specifics
below.

## Primary goal

**Get a quote / book service.** Dominant CTA: "Get a Free Quote" or "Book
Service". Phone CTA must be persistent — trade buyers often pick up the
phone. Sticky "Call Now" button on mobile (bottom of screen).

## Conversion levers

Speed-to-response is the entire game:

- "We respond in 1 hour"
- "Same-day service available"
- "Emergency? Call now"
- "Free estimates" (no obligation)
- License + insured (compliance signals)

## Section order

1. **Sticky nav** — logo, "Get Quote" button right, "Call: XXX-XXX-XXXX"
   visible. On mobile, phone is the most prominent action.
2. **Hero** — split layout: technician/work photo on one side; H1 + "Get
   a Free Quote in 60 seconds" subhead + quote form (name + phone +
   service type) on the other. Below the form: trust row — "Licensed",
   "Insured", "5-Star Rated", "Same-Day".
3. **Service area band** — "Serving {city} and surrounding areas". Map
   or a simple zip-code lookup when available.
4. **Services grid** — Emergency Service / Free Estimates / Preventive
   Maintenance / Residential / Commercial / Warranty. Real before/after
   photos if available.
5. **Reviews** — verbatim from `placesReviews`. Lean into specific story
   reviews ("they came at 11pm and fixed our heat") over generic praise.
6. **How it works** — 3 steps: Call/Submit → Free Estimate → Job Done.
   Keep it punchy.
7. **License & credentials** — license number, BBB rating, manufacturer
   certifications, years in business. This is a real compliance signal
   for the buyer.
8. **FAQ** — pricing transparency, scheduling, warranty, emergency
   protocol.
9. **Final CTA band** — bold contrasting color. "Need it fixed today?
   Call now or get a free estimate." + click-to-call + form link.
10. **Footer** — name, license number, service area zips, phone, hours.

## Style notes

- **High contrast, bold.** Trade buyers respond to clarity and confidence,
  not subtlety. Heavy weights, generous tap targets, primary CTA color
  should dominate the page.
- **Sans-serif always.** Serif reads "agency", not "trade".
- **Photos must show work**, not stock — technician with gear, branded
  truck, completed install. Stock photos read fake.
- **Mobile-first.** Trade leads come from mobile search. Test the page
  at 375px width mentally as you build.

## What must NEVER appear

- Long marketing copy about "our values" — trade buyers skip it
- Vague pricing ("Affordable rates") — either show real numbers or omit
- Stock photos of suit-wearing executives shaking hands
- Newsletter signup
- "Schedule a consultation" — too soft. Use "Get a Quote" or "Book Service"
