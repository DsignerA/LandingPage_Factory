# landing-page-factory-lawyer

Follow the **landing-page-factory** base contract. Professional-services
layer below.

## Primary goal

**Schedule a consultation.** Dominant CTA: "Schedule Free Consultation" or
"Book a Free Strategy Call". Secondary: "Call us". Tertiary: download
a guide / case study (lead magnet) when the brief mentions one.

## Conversion levers

Professional services sell **authority + trust**:

- Years of experience
- Case results / win rate (when verifiable from scraped data)
- Bar admissions / certifications
- Recognitions and press mentions
- Confidentiality assurance
- Free first consultation, no obligation

## Section order

1. **Sticky nav** — logo, links (Practice Areas / About / Results / FAQ /
   Contact), "Free Consultation" button right.
2. **Hero** — centered or split layout. Avoid stock-photo-of-suited-people.
   Prefer office interior, founder portrait, or law-library photo. H1 is
   a confident positioning statement (from brief's scraped tagline).
   Subhead = the brand's elevator pitch. Two CTAs: "Schedule Consultation"
   + "Call".
3. **Trust strip** — Bar admissions, years in practice, awards, peer-review
   ratings (AVVO, Martindale-Hubbell) — only when scraped data confirms.
4. **Practice areas grid** — 4–8 cards (Personal Injury / Family Law /
   Estate Planning / etc.). Each card: title + one-sentence positioning
   + "Learn More". Don't list services as bullet lists — cards convert
   better.
5. **About / our story** — split layout: founder portrait if available +
   1–2 paragraphs from scraped about. Emphasize tenure and personal
   investment in client outcomes.
6. **Results / case studies** — only when scraped data has specific
   results. Three concise wins with disclaimer ("past results don't
   guarantee future outcomes"). Skip entirely if the brief has no real
   case results — never fabricate.
7. **Reviews / testimonials** — first-name + last-initial only (privacy).
   Quote-card layout, not star ratings (too retail).
8. **How it works** — 3 steps: Free Consultation → Case Review →
   Representation Strategy.
9. **Contact form section** — name, phone, email, brief description of
   matter. Reassure confidentiality.
10. **FAQ** — fees ("What does representation cost?"), confidentiality,
    timeline, jurisdiction.
11. **Final CTA band** — calm, restrained color. "Talk through your
    situation in a free 30-minute call." + form / phone.
12. **Footer** — name, address, phone, bar admissions list, disclaimer
    ("Attorney advertising. Past results do not guarantee future outcomes."),
    privacy policy link, ADA accessibility statement link.

## Style notes

- **Restrained, editorial typography.** Serif headings, generous leading,
  conservative color use. Trust comes from restraint.
- **Avoid emoji and exclamation points entirely.**
- **Long-form copy is acceptable** for professional services — buyers
  research extensively. Don't truncate the About section.
- **Confidentiality framing throughout.** "Confidential consultation",
  "your information stays with us".

## Required legal language

Always include:
- "Attorney advertising" in the footer (legal compliance in most US states)
- "Past results do not guarantee future outcomes" near any case results
- "This site does not establish an attorney-client relationship" — small,
  near the contact form

## What must NEVER appear

- Specific case-result numbers without scraped sourcing
- Photos of obvious stock lawyers
- Marketing pop-ups, urgency language ("Limited consultations available!")
- Sales-style countdown timers
- "Risk-free guarantee" or any unconditional success claim
- Bar admissions for jurisdictions not in the scraped data
