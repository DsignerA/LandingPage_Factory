Our Story

Three generations,
two cities, one table.

The room is dim, the leather is broken in, and the kitchen still answers to the same question every generation has asked: is it perfect yet?

Good Questions

Before you arrive.

Do you take reservations?
Yes. Reservations are the best way to ensure a table on weekends and during peak season. Walk-ins are welcome at the bar when space allows. Call (804) 643-6900 to book.
Is there a dress code?
Smart casual is the room's natural tempo — a collared shirt or equivalent, comfortable closed-toe shoes. Jackets are welcome but not required.
Where do guests park?
Street parking is available along East Cary Street, with paid lots within a short walk. Please call ahead for valet availability during private events.
Can you host a private event?
Yes. We host rehearsal dinners, anniversaries, corporate gatherings, and private celebrations of every size. Call (804) 643-6900 and ask for the events team.
Do you accommodate dietary requests?
Almost always. Tell us when you reserve and the kitchen will plan around allergies, vegetarian preferences, or specific celebrations. The wine list adapts too.
Do you offer take-out?
Select dishes from the steakhouse menu are available for take-out by phone. Call (804) 643-6900 to place an order; please allow 30–40 minutes during dinner service.
